How to use this PC

This is {{COMPUTER}}, one of four identical workstations in the CBR Facility: JOHN, PAUL, GEORGE and RINGO. They have the same apps at the same versions, so a project that opens on one opens on the others.

First: this PC isn't backed up. Keep a copy of anything that matters somewhere else, and copy your work off at the end of every session.

Signed in as CBR Session? This account is wiped clean after each session: files, browser history and app sign-ins. Save anything you want to keep to your own drive or OneDrive before you leave.

Where to keep your work

  • While you're working: the Work folder on your desktop. It's on this PC's fast drive, which Unreal, Unity, RealityScan and LCC need.
  • Documents, renders and exports: your university OneDrive. With your own account on this PC, the OneDrive app is there: sign in with your university email. Files only download when you open them, so they don't fill the drive. On the shared accounts, use OneDrive in the browser.
  • Big projects (engine projects, scans, 360 footage): your own external SSD, copied at the end of each session.
  • Code and engine projects: GitHub (with Git LFS for big files) or Unity Version Control, so you have the history as well as a copy.

Signing in to apps

  • Sign in to Epic Games, Unity Hub and Meta Quest Developer Hub with your own accounts. Epic and Unity are free: sign up with your university email.
  • Don't let browsers save your passwords.
  • Sign out of anything personal before you leave. Someone else uses this PC next.

What you can't change, and why

You can't install software, change drivers or change system settings. That keeps all four PCs the same, so your work runs on any of them.

  • Need an app, or a different version? Ask the CBR Tech team (below). It goes on all four PCs.
  • Plugins inside your project are fine: Unreal or Unity plugins, Blender add-ons, Python packages installed with uv.
  • Please don't update the NVIDIA driver, Unreal or Unity yourself. Their versions are fixed so projects don't break.

Disk space

Each account has a limit on this PC. You'll get a message when you're getting close: copy finished work off and delete it here, so you don't run out in the middle of saving something.

Heavy jobs

  • This PC has an RTX 5090 with 32 GB of graphics memory: big renders, simulations and AI work are what it's for.
  • Running something overnight? Book it with the CBR Tech team and leave a note on the screen. Lock the PC (Windows + L) rather than signing out: signing out stops your job. Only you can unlock it, so please don't lock it and leave unless it's booked.
  • AI and Linux work: the first time, run Code > Set up Ubuntu & Docker (about 15 minutes, once). After that, open Code > Ubuntu. Docker can use the GPU: docker run --gpus all …

Headsets, scanners and cameras

  • Meta Quest: plug in by USB-C, then use Meta Quest Developer Hub or SideQuest.
  • XGRIDS scans: process them in LCC Studio; LCC Editor and LCC Model Editor are in the same folder.
  • Insta360 footage: import and edit in Insta360 Studio.
  • VIVE Mars and other kit: ask the CBR Tech team.

When you've finished

  1. Copy your work off this PC.
  2. Close your apps. Unsaved work is lost when you sign out.
  3. Sign out of personal accounts in browsers and apps.
  4. Sign out of Windows: Start > your name > Sign out. Please don't shut down: the PCs update themselves.

Help

Restarting the app, then the PC, fixes a lot. You only lose unsaved work.

Still stuck, need an app, or new to the CBR Facility? Email cbr@falmouth.ac.uk, or speak to the CBR Tech team in the CBR Facility, on the ground floor of Launchpad. For a problem, tell us three things: this PC's name ({{COMPUTER}}), what you were doing, and a screenshot of any error (Windows + Shift + S).
